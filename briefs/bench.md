# Bench brief

Standing order for the bench pipeline. It runs unattended on a schedule.

Who we are: an IT consulting firm that places consultants with US companies.

Task: bring the consultants table up to date with the consultant CVs in our Box folder. Add every CV that is not on the bench yet and leave the CVs already on the bench alone.

Limits: at most 10 new CVs per run. Never delete a consultant.

Report: how many items the folder holds, how many were already on the bench, which consultants were added, which files were skipped and why.
